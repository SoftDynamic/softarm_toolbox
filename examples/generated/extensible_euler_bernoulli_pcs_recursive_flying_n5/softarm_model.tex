\section*{Recursive Soft-Arm Dynamics}
The Exact-SE(3) inverse-dynamics routine propagates section boundary velocities and accelerations forward and pulls spatial wrenches backward. The mass matrix is obtained by unit-acceleration inverse-dynamics calls, and $h(q,\dot q)$ by a zero-acceleration call.
