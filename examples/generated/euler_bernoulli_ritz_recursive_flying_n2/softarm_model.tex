\section*{Recursive Soft-Arm Dynamics}
The displacement-Ritz model propagates a global first-order affine transform jet while retaining the full nonlinear floating-base transform and base--arm mixed terms. Arm--arm coordinate products are discarded. Fixed Gauss quadrature directly assembles the mass matrix, Coriolis, gravity, elastic, and damping terms. Forward and KKT dynamics solve the resulting linear systems without forming an inverse.
